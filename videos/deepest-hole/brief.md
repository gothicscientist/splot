# What if you kept digging straight down?

- mode: what-if
- style: blockcraft (original voxel; no game assets, never the game's name in titles or tags)
- platform: tiktok + reels + shorts
- length: 41.9 s, 11 beats, 6 shots, ElevenLabs take (voice "Liam"), captions `chunky`
- audience: curious 13-35 viewers of voxel-game and science shorts; zero prior knowledge assumed
- the one thing the viewer should feel/learn: humanity's deepest hole is tiny (a dinner plate wide) and barely
  scratched the planet (0.2 % of the way to the centre), because hot rock stops behaving like rock.
- reference formats (outliers) and what we copy from them (structure, hook pattern, pacing):
  - The "what happens if you..." consequence chain (search median ~2.1M views; Zack D-style hypotheticals, 6.4M channel
    median; `docs/research/B-formats-modes.md` §2.2). We copy: second-person question hook, escalation step by step
    (1 km, 5, 10, record), a twist (rock turns plastic), a number payoff that reframes the whole thing (0.2 %).
  - Voxel cross-section "dig down" visuals from the genre: the ground cut open, layers and ores, a depth readout. We
    copy the device (a cutaway with a counter), with our own blocks, characters and UI.
- what we never copy: words, footage, characters, the game's textures, UI or logo.
