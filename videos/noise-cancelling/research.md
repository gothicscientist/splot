## Conflicts to watch

Flagged only: the voice is recorded, so the video's wording stays as it is.

- Row 4 (b05 "Peak meets valley, the two waves cancel, and you hear almost nothing"): BBC Science Focus
  (https://www.sciencefocus.com/science/how-do-noise-cancelling-headphones-cancel-sounds) backs the mechanism: peak
  meets trough and the waves cancel by destructive interference. On the result it is softer: ANC cannot remove unwanted
  sound completely, and "around 70 per cent of ambient noise can be blocked using this method". That supports "much
  less" (the row's wording), not "almost nothing". The next beats (b06-b09) add the catch that voices get through,
  which limits the overclaim. A source showing near-total cancellation of a steady, low hum would clear this flag.
