# How noise-cancelling headphones work

- mode: how-it-works
- style: glowvector (original glow-lit flat vector; not Kurzgesagt's characters, logo or music)
- platform: tiktok + reels + shorts
- length: 39.6 s, 11 beats, 7 shots, ElevenLabs take (voice "Liam"), captions `clean`
- audience: everyday headphone owners 16-45, commuters and travellers; no physics background assumed
- the one thing the viewer should feel/learn: noise cancelling makes sound (a flipped copy of the noise) and wins
  only on steady low hums; the padding has to stop sudden, high sounds such as voices.
- reference formats (outliers) and what we copy from them (structure, hook pattern, pacing):
  - Zack D-style mechanism explainers ("How A Gun Silencer Actually Works" 52.5M, "How An Inhaler Works" 50M, about
    30 s, ~170 wpm; `docs/research/B-formats-modes.md` §2.3). We copy: paradox hook ("they make more noise"), the
    x-ray inside view one part at a time (mic, chip, speaker), a "but there's a catch" turn, a payoff line that
    reframes the everyday object.
  - One colour per idea for the whole film (coral = noise, cyan = anti-noise, gold = voice) so the diagram explains
    itself without labels.
- what we never copy: words, characters, mascots, music or the look of any specific channel.
