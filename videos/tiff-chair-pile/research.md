# Research: My chair hasn't been a chair since March

Facts about the product (all from the product's own site, checked 2026-11-15):
- pileGo! lists clothes "everywhere you sell": eBay, Vinted, Depop, Poshmark, Mercari, Grailed (6 marketplaces).
  Source: https://pilego.app/ (marketplace list on the home page).
- "When one sells, it comes down everywhere else." Source: https://pilego.app/ (hero copy).
- "Snap the clothes piling up on your chair and pileGo! lists them everywhere you sell." Source: https://pilego.app/
- Free and open source. Source: https://pilego.app/ and https://pilego.app/extension ("Free and open source").
- Price suggestions use eBay sold listings ("reads eBay's sold listings so price suggestions use what things actually
  sold for"). Source: https://pilego.app/extension
- Real photos and cutouts, no AI recreations. Source: https://pilego.app/
- Works through a browser extension on Chrome, Edge, Brave or Firefox on a computer (Vinted in one click, a filled-in
  sell form on the others). Source: https://pilego.app/extension. Scripts say "lists it everywhere" (the site's own
  wording) and make no speed or earnings claims.

Story beats are a fictional skit with the recurring cartoon lead Tiff (not a testimonial). No statistics are used.
Crosslisting (one item listed on several marketplaces) and the risk of selling the same item twice when it is not
removed elsewhere are general reseller practice described in the product's own pitch.
