# Resale culture and seller pain (niche bible, as of 2026-11-15)

> What Depop, Vinted, eBay, Poshmark, Mercari and Grailed sellers live through and joke about, what survived a fact check, and where pileGo! fits. For the script writer and critic of every Tiff or ug-rap episode.

Fact-checked on 2026-11-15. The owner has permission for pileGo!'s automatic relisting, so platform automation rules are background only.

## In one minute
- Every app rewards new or active listings, so sellers re-post by hand. Vinted counts upload age [24], Depop gives new items an edge [17], Grailed bumps are free only for 30 days [31], and Poshmark sharing lifts items [34]. pileGo! ends that grind.
- The evergreen laughs are the lowballer (the shared villain) and the clothes chair (the shared shame, and pileGo!'s premise).
- Fees move, so cite one only with its date. Depop (UK/US/AU) and Vinted charge sellers nothing. Depop Boost charges 12 % if a buyer who merely saw the tile buys within 28 days. Grailed charges 6 % under $120 and 9 % from $120.
- Vinted's buyer charge is now the "Vinted fee" (Oct 2026). eBay has owned Depop since 30 Jul 2026.
- "Archive" is resale's most abused word. "Ballhog" is the new one, hashtag-stacked onto plain tees and rapper merch.
- Honesty is the insider flex. Depop and Vinted punish brand names you aren't selling, and most US Gen Z trust listings with AI-made photos less. pileGo!'s "real photos, real cutouts, no AI recreations" lands here.
- Photo-to-listing AI is standard on eBay, Depop and Poshmark [22, 35, 39]. pileGo!'s edge is the pile, five apps at once, and the item gone everywhere once it sells. A double sale is a real fear [21].
- Tiff sells her own pile: no reps, bootlegs or off-app deals. She is 23; the Ballhog crowd skews teen. pileGo! enters after ~70 % of the runtime. Platform names appear as plain text, never as logos.

## The relisting grind
Narrator line: "Every resale app pushes whatever's newest, so sellers re-post the same clothes on every app, one by one, forever."

| App | What it rewards (its own pages) | Manual workaround | Src |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vinted | Upload age and interactions. It promotes new and Bumped items, shows bursts of uploads in proportion, and gives a badge for N listings in 30 days | Re-upload, Bump, list in small batches | 24, 29 |
| Depop | Relevance and popularity (views, likes, adds to bag); new items get a small edge | Relist (our inference: the copy restarts at zero likes) | 17 |
| Grailed | A free bump every 7 days for 30 days, then only with a price cut of 10 % or more | Weekly bumps, then 10 % cuts | 31 |
| Poshmark | Shares push items up search and feeds (2016 guidance; today's weight is disputed) | Share the closet daily | 34 |

A Vinted listing held for its automated check usually waits 15-30 minutes, sometimes 1-4 days [29]. No independent time-cost figure exists, and vendors' "hours saved" claims are marketing. So show it instead: 1 a.m., thumb cramp, the same jeans for the fourth time. eBay and Mercari were not checked.

## Story and joke angles
pileGo! never appears before ~70 % of the runtime.

1. **The chair has lore.** A museum tour of her clothes chair; the placards say "archive, worn twice". Lands: everyone has a chair, and archive inflation is the scene's favourite eye-roll. Fit check. pileGo!: punchline at ~80 %: she snaps the pile, and the chair turns out to be green.
2. **Lowball boss fight.** A JRPG battle: "£4?" on a £60 tee, and her menu reads Counter / Decline / Block / Write a diss track. Lands: everyone has fought this monster, and the diss option is ug-coded. POV skit or comment-reply series. pileGo!: punchline: it already sold elsewhere, so the attack hits nothing.
3. **Two buyers, one hoodie.** It sells on Depop and Vinted four minutes apart; heist split-screen. Lands: the cross-lister's nightmare, and Depop makes you refund. POV skit (heist). pileGo!: punchline at ~75 %: the second listing has already vanished.
4. **1 a.m. relist ritual.** Candles, chanting, 60 deletes and re-posts, thumb in a cast. Lands: "new gets seen" is folklore that Vinted half-confirms. POV skit. pileGo!: self-aware ad beat: "Or. One click."
5. **Boost tax.** She learns that a buyer only has to see her Boosted tile for the 12 % to count once they buy, so she asks buyers to shut their eyes. Lands: it's the real rule. Storytime. pileGo!: self-aware ad beat: "pileGo! can't fix that. It's free, though."
6. **48 hours of terror.** She refreshes a delivered order like she's waiting for a text back. It completes: confetti, ad-lib. Lands: every Vinted seller's dread. POV skit. pileGo!: habit: she snaps the next pile while she waits.
7. **DM drama theatre.** An invented buyer chat performed as Shakespeare: "Is this still available?" on a live Buy-now listing, then "bundle?". Lands: DM drama is a genre, and a Marketplace opener on Vinted is wrong-app comedy. Storytime. pileGo!: punchline: "If it's up, it's available."
8. **Archive court.** A friend's mall-brand jeans are titled RARE ARCHIVE BALLHOG GRAIL, and Judge Tiff reads it into the record. Lands: 38,000 "archive" listings, and #ballhog on plain tees. Roast. pileGo!: punchline: her own listing is a real photo, a real cutout and a plain title.
9. **Binge bale.** Mid-Vinted-binge she buys an "archive grade" bale: 40 lanyards and one gem she guards like a relic. Lands: the addiction talk plus the bale gamble. POV skit (unboxing parody). pileGo!: habit: everything but the gem goes on the pile, then into pileGo!.
10. **Photo day.** Golden-hour mirror pic, tape measure, close-ups of the flaws, then the cat lies on the jeans. Lands: every seller knows photo day. Fit check. pileGo!: punchline: real photo, real cutout: clean jeans, offended cat left behind.
11. **(ug-rap) Merch archaeology.** The narrator reads a rapper's era through resale listings: tour long-sleeves, handprinted fan tees, a store-brand hoodie "worn by" the artist. Lands: fans know the merch market and its keyword games. Storytime. pileGo!: a self-aware ad beat by the narrator at the end, never tied to the artist.

## Cringe, poser tells and hard avoids
What gets clowned in the comments:
- Calling mall brands "archive" or "grail" with a straight face.
- Wrong basics: Vinted charging sellers, "Etsy's Depop", mixing up Bump and Boost, treating "Is this still available?" as a Vinted thing.
- Tiff's own listings stuffed with hashtags or unrelated brands. Leave that to side characters.
- Tiff selling reps, bootlegs or "inspired" merch, or moving a deal to PayPal or WhatsApp. That's scammer-coded.
- AI-made product photos, which clash with the real-photos pitch.
- Invented numbers ("triples sales", "saves 10 hours") or fees without a date.
- The ad beat before ~70 %, or twice; glamorising compulsive buying.

Legal fences:
- No platform or brand logos and no copied app screens; use generic UI. Names in plain text are fine. Clothes are invented, with no real logos; brands appear only in dialogue.
- No real person's likeness or private info. Never show real sellers' or buyers' chats, listings or handles.
- No real person shown or implied to endorse pileGo! (rappers, Bradygoo, creators).
- Tiff reads clearly as an adult (23) and is never in school settings. Vinted is 18+, and the UK is weighing an under-16 ban on resale apps [5].
- No jokes about dodging tax.
