# Tiff and ug-rap story formats (niche bible, as of 2026-11-15)

> What wins with the archive-fashion and underground-rap audience, how pileGo! shows up without killing the joke, and how to post it. For the script writer, the critic and the poster of every Tiff or ug-rap episode.

Checked 2026-11-15 (check log below). View counts come from that day's TikTok topic pages [36]: each is a single source, and the pages rotate. Adds to `posting.md`.

## In one minute
- **What wins:** in-group comedy: insider reveals, the scene roasting its own elitism, and resale pain. On 15 Nov, a "larpers" self-roast had 1.9M views, numbered Vinted POVs posted as comment replies had 2.2M and 2.1M, and a satire of men quizzing a girl had 323K [36a,f,i].
- **Frame 1:** one premise in one overlay, plus Tiff's face. Weakening openers: "come with me", "watch until the end", a generic "wait for it", "you need this" [33].
- **pileGo!:** only after ~70 % of the runtime, as a 1-3 s punchline inside the pain or as Tiff's habit. Name it once and close on a real question. Never "like for part 2": TikTok makes posts that push for likes or follows ineligible for its For You feed (FYF) [4].
- **Tiff:** obviously drawn, never passed off as real. She is attractive through her face, hair, attitude and fit, with no body-focused framing, and she is clearly an adult [5].
- **TikTok disclosure:** turn on content disclosure and pick **Your brand** on every episode. The label reads "Promotional content" and has no effect on reach. Once posted it can't be changed; to fix it, delete and repost [1].
- **AI labels:** TikTok needs **none** for generic text-to-speech (TTS) narration or anime styles. It needs one for realistic people or scenes, or for a voice that mimics a real person [4]. Instagram **requires** "Add AI label" on a Reel narrated by a realistic AI voiceover [11].
- **Music:** only our own code-generated score. TikTok's licences outside its Commercial Music Library (CML) exclude commercial use [3], and Instagram's licensed library is for personal use only [16].
- **Brand link:** pileGo! goes in the handle and bio, plus once on screen and once in the voice. If the handle is the character's name only, add "ad" up front [19,20,22,23].
- **Real people:** never shown, voiced or implied to approve pileGo! [4,24]. In ug-rap, the product beat belongs to the fans.
- **"Ballhog" is at peak crossover:** explainers ran from 13 Oct to 4 Nov 2026, and YPulse warns it may fade [26-29]. Build on the mechanic (a plain look worth a lot to insiders), not the word.

## How pileGo! shows up without killing the joke
**Facts only**, from the owner's brief:
- pileGo! (pilego.app), "Clear the pile. Keep the cash."
- Snap the pile on your chair and it lists the items wherever you sell (eBay, Vinted, Depop, Poshmark, Mercari).
- When one item sells, it comes down everywhere else.
- Free, open source, a browser extension, one-click AI-powered relisting.

No speed or earnings claims and no testimonials, since fake ones are illegal [21].

**Why it works this way:** Duo (Duolingo's mascot) has 18M+ TikTok followers, credited to a consistent voice that joins conversations already happening, and a Gen Z founder says her generation hates overt ads [34]. Proof beats recommendation, and the product works as the setting of the joke [33].

| pattern | rule | original example | share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Punchline inside the pain | The joke builds the exact pain; the app ends it in 1-3 s. No feature list, no pitch voice | He's still typing "is this still av-" when her phone pings: sold on another app, gone from the rest. She doesn't look up | ~70 % |
| Defining habit | A Duolingo-style trait: she can't let a pile exist | At a friend's flat she photographs the chair pile like a crime scene: "evidence. it's going live." | Running gag |
| Ad parody | The brand mocks its own advert | A glossy voice starts "introducing a revolution in-"; Tiff pulls the plug: "it lists your stuff everywhere and pulls it when one sells. it's free. bye." | At most once a month |
| Prop | The app on her phone, with no comment | A "live on 5 sites" toast while she argues with chat | Light |

**Timing:** the app's name or UI first appears at 70 % of the runtime or later: 10.5 s into 15, 17.5 into 25, 31.5 into 45, 63 into 90. The unnamed habit can come earlier.

**Closers (original):** "accept the £4, counter or block?" / "guess the year on the tank" / "keep, relist or burn: the grey hoodie?"

## Posting compliance checklist (before every post, both channels)
This refines `posting.md` §7.3. Under TikTok's guidelines effective 24 Sep 2026, generic TTS needs no label. "A voice that could pass as real" means a voice that mimics a real person.

**TikTok**
- [ ] Content disclosure on and **Your brand** chosen on every Tiff and ug-rap episode; the label reads "Promotional content". If the wrong option was picked, delete and repost. Undisclosed promotion can lose visibility, and repeats can lead to posting limits or a ban [1,6].
- [ ] Audio is only our score and voice. Tick the Music Usage Confirmation (true: there is no third-party music). No TikTok library sounds; the CML is the only fallback [3,8].
- [ ] AIGC label stays off when the only AI element is a stock TTS narrator that isn't a known person's recognisable voice. Turn it on for any realistic AI shot. No cloned or celebrity-like voices [2,4].
  - Our pick is to disclose the synthetic voice in the bio instead. Labelling drawn, non-AI visuals invites the "AI slop" read, and it is unverified how TikTok's "see less AI" setting treats labelled posts [10].
- [ ] No like-for-like or follow-to-unlock lines [4]. Comment episodes go up as reply-with-video. Post only through the official app or official tools; TikTok bans automation that bypasses its systems [4].

**Instagram**
- [ ] **Add AI label** turned on, before sharing, for every Reel or Story with TTS narration. It is required, and penalties are possible [11].
- [ ] No Paid partnership tag on our own posts. It is only for a paid collaborator's post or for affiliate links [15].
- [ ] Original audio only. The licensed library is non-commercial and some business accounts lack it; Sound Collection is the commercial-safe option [16].
- [ ] At most 5 hashtags, an on-topic caption, and a Reel of 3 min or less so it stays recommendable [14,17].
- [ ] AI-generated profile label not required (our reading): Tiff is drawn, not a photorealistic AI person [12,14]. Check Account Status weekly, and if the account is flagged, add the label (it doesn't change reach) [12].

**Law (US, UK, EU) and house fences**
- [ ] The brand link is obvious. Default: a handle containing "pilego"; a bio whose first line reads "pileGo!'s animated character, synthetic voice"; pileGo! named once on screen and once in the voice. With a character-only handle, put "ad" first in the caption and on screen at the product beat [19,20,22,23].
- [ ] Tiff speaks as the advertiser's own character (an advertiser's spokesperson is not an endorser [18]). She is never an independent user who "found" the app. The UK ad regulator (ASA) counts a "virtually produced persona" as an influencer [23].
- [ ] No real person is shown, voiced or implied to use or approve pileGo!. In ug-rap, the product beats go to fans or the narrator. No real person is portrayed adversely in a promo video [4,24]. Facts are sourced in `videos/<slug>/research.md`.
- [ ] Claims come only from the facts list [21].
- [ ] EU: no deepfakes (no real person's likeness or voice). Machine-marking is the TTS provider's duty; systems already on the market have until 2 Dec 2026. The owner reviews every script before posting, and that human editorial control exempts it from the AI-text label [25,37].
- [ ] The framing table passes, a fresh critic confirms Tiff reads as an adult, and no third-party logos or app UI are drawn.

## Story and joke angles
pileGo! never appears before ~70 % of the runtime.
1. **Merch-table quiz** (F4). A guy quizzes Tiff on deep cuts. She answers with leak dates, then asks what he has actually listed: 40 tagged pieces in a drawer. It reverses a live satire. pileGo!: at ~75 % she snaps the drawer, and it goes live on five sites.
2. **The plain tank** (F1). Her default fit is priced piece by piece; only the boots are cheap. pileGo!: at ~80 % she relists the piece she's bored of.
3. **The £4 man, pt 1-n** (F6). One lowballer, a new offer each part, each part answering a real comment. pileGo!: every part ends with the item sold elsewhere and gone from his screen.
4. **Double-sold** (F8). One jacket sells on two apps 40 s apart; panicked DMs; then "how it should've gone". pileGo!: the second listing comes down by itself.
5. **Types at the show** (F3). The larper, the clone, the spreadsheet guy ("87 % accurate"), the old head, the reseller who listed his tour tee before the encore, and Tiff. pileGo!: the reseller, at ~80 %.
6. **Obscurity ladder** (F5). Her taste climbs to an act with 11 monthly listeners who drops only in a group chat; her listing titles read like router passwords. pileGo!: they still go live everywhere.
7. **The chair pile gets a lease** (storytime). The pile grows a name and opinions, then charges rent. pileGo!: one snap and it moves out.
8. **Chat decides** (F9). "tuff or Walmart ballhog?" Chat spams "list it" until she gives in.
9. **Dad found the word.** He explains ballhog to her from an article. pileGo!: she quietly relists everything an adult has heard of.
10. **Keep, relist, burn** (F10). She rates three pieces described in a comment, never from a viewer's photo.
11. **ug-rap: merch afterlife.** A sourced breakout story. The coda follows an original fan whose tour tee from that era now resells. pileGo!: the fan lists it at ≥80 %. The artist is never drawn and never near the app.
12. **ug-rap: first show.** Original stand-in fans at a show: the merch line, the pit, a lost hoodie. pileGo!: the pile at home afterwards.

## Cringe, poser tells and hard avoids
| poser tells (house) | hard avoids (legal, platform) |
|---|---|
| Explaining the meme inside the joke; brand-worn slang used sincerely | No trademarked logos or app UI drawn; name pieces on cards or in dialogue [4] |
| More than 2 slang words, or misused slang | No real person's likeness, voice, photo or private info, including viral reaction photos |
| A polished ad look, logo intros, weak openers [33] | No real person shown or implied endorsing anything [4,24] |
| Wrong-era silhouettes (accuracy earns respect) | No real songs, lyrics, beats, soundalikes or library sounds [3,16] |
| A misogynistic meme tone; youth-coded settings | No other creators' clips, concert footage or watermarks: they lose FYF eligibility and Instagram recommendations [4,14] |
| Begging for engagement | pileGo! never lists reps as genuine (promoting counterfeits is banned [7]); no fake testimonials [21]; copy mechanics, never lines |
