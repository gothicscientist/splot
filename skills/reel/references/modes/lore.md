## MODE 6 — LORE EXPLAINED (anime, manga, games, films, series)

**Purpose.** Make a fan feel they finally understand a rule, power, secret or backstory in their franchise, or give a newcomer the whole plot in 60 s.

Sub-types:

- **Power math / rule explained** (Silenthys: "The math behind Gojo's Infinity", 5.7M)
- **Superlative countdown** (Ohara: "The Strongest Marine In One Piece EVER!", 13.2M)
- **Rating card** (Ohara: "Rating Kizaru's Light Devil Fruit", 7.5M)
- **Plot in 60 s** (Ohara: "One Piece In 60 Seconds", 12M)
- **Backstory / why X turned evil** (search median for "anime villain backstory" is 872K)
- **"In every game"** lists (ITalkFNAF)
- **Hidden-lore reveal** ("Did you know Temple Run might actually have lore?", 12.2M)

**Input.** Franchise plus a question, character, power or arc, plus a spoiler ceiling (e.g. "anime only" / "up to ch. 1100").

**Research steps:**

1. Use the official wiki (Fandom), chapter/episode citations, official databooks and creator interviews. Separate canon from theory.
2. Check the **latest episode or chapter** for timeliness. Release weeks spike demand; StojDoes and Silenthys uploads land during airing.
3. For power math, compute the numbers explicitly. Example: Black Flash = damage to the power of 2.5, "A punch worth 10 becomes 300."
4. Prepare a spoiler warning if it goes beyond the anime.
5. **IP safety:**
   - No clips, screenshots, official art or music.
   - Use original stand-ins: silhouettes or stick figures with signature props and colours (straw hat, blindfold), plus original diagrams.
   - Rights-holder enforcement on YouTube, TikTok and IG is intensifying in 2026.

**Script template, 45 s (~165 words at ~215 wpm):**

| t (s) | Beat | Script job | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | HOOK | Paradox or superlative ("The strongest punch in Jujutsu Kaisen is the one nobody can do on purpose.") | Character stand-in in action pose; title card with the power name |
| 3–10 | DEFINE | What the thing is, in one line, plus its rule | Character card (name, faction colour, rank); rule written as an equation or diagram |
| 10–25 | MECHANICS | 2–3 escalating facts with numbers or comparisons ("within 1 millionth of a second… sprinters need a tenth…") | Number counters, scale comparisons, timeline bars |
| 25–35 | TWIST | The canon detail most fans missed, or the countdown reveal ("…but it's none of the Admirals") | Silhouette reveal; manga-style speed lines; source stamp ("Ch. 1093") |
| 35–45 | PAYOFF + PROMPT | Final number, score, or verdict + debate question / "Follow for more [franchise] explained" | Score meter filling, or a versus split; comment prompt text |

**75 s extension:** plot-in-60 (one beat per 3–4 s, 15–18 beats), or a full rating card. Rating card layout: power 25 + versatility 25 + awakening 25 + weakness deduction → "a whopping 35 points" / "82 out of 100… number one for now".

**Hook templates:**

1. "The [strongest / most broken] [thing] in [franchise] is the one [paradox]." (Silenthys, 2.1M; also "the fastest way to get stronger is to make yourself weaker", 1.2M)
2. "This is the strongest [rank] in [franchise] of all time, and it's someone no one would have expected." (Ohara, 13.2M)
3. "I'm rating every [power / devil fruit / weapon] in [franchise] to figure out which one's the best." (Ohara, 7.5M)

Also:

- "Did you know [franchise] actually has [hidden lore]?" (Rocket Riley "Did you know Earth actually exists in Star Wars canon?", 12.3M)
- "What is the scariest story in the [franchise] books?" (ITalkFNAF, 7.1M)

**Best art styles.**

- Stick-figure / silhouette stand-ins with iconic props in a stylised arena
- Game-UI stat cards and meters (Ohara)
- Infographic/diagram for power math
- 2D cel-shaded original characters only if clearly not copies

**Visual conventions.**

- Character card on first mention.
- Faction colour coding.
- Numbers always visualised.
- Countdown elimination: cross out each wrong guess.
- Spoiler banner.
- Rating meter at the end.

**Voice and audio.**

- Hype fan energy, fast (195–250 wpm; FNAF lore channels hit 250–283).
- Music: original epic/trap-orchestral; impact hits on reveals.

**Ending.** Score or verdict plus a debate prompt, or a "follow for more" CTA (Silenthys ends: "Follow Silenthys for more anime explained").

**Common mistakes.**

- Using clips or official art (claims and strikes).
- Wrong canon (fans correct you in the comments).
- Spoilers without warning.
- Slow pace.
- Overly long recap with no twist.

**Why it wins.**

- Identity fandom.
- "Finally understand" payoff.
- Release-week spikes.
- Debate fuels comments.

The caveat is that the channel median is low unless the channel has a signature device, such as rating cards, power math or memes.

**Pattern.** Paradox or superlative, or a "did you know [franchise] has [hidden thing]" line.

**Length and pace.** 46–67 s at 195–283 wpm.

**Retention.**

- Countdown elimination ("what about the Admirals… none of the Admirals…").
- Number escalation ("A punch worth 10 becomes 300. A punch worth 100 becomes 100,000.").
- Score reveal.

**Ending.** Score, a debate prompt, or "Follow for more".

**Mistakes.** Clips or official art; theory sold as canon; spoilers; generic "explained" titles.
